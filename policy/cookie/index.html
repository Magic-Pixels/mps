Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on magicpixels.shop.

Consent comes first

Analytics is optional. Google Analytics is not downloaded and no Analytics data is sent until you explicitly accept the Analytics category in the cookie banner. Rejecting optional cookies does not restrict access to the website. Continuing to browse, scrolling or closing the banner does not count as consent.

Technologies in use

Necessary consent storage. CookieYes remembers whether you accepted or rejected categories. This is required to preserve your choice and operate the consent controls.

Analytics cookies. If you accept Analytics, Google Analytics may set cookies beginning with _ga. They distinguish visits and help us understand which pages and traffic sources are useful. Advertising storage, Google Signals and ad personalization are disabled.

Affiliate links. Product links lead to third-party merchants or affiliate networks. Those services may use their own cookies after you choose to leave this site. Their notices and controls apply on their websites.

Change or withdraw your choice

Cookie settings

You can reopen the settings at any time, reject Analytics, or withdraw a previous choice. When Analytics consent is withdrawn, this site disables Analytics, removes accessible Analytics cookies and reloads without the Google tag.

You can also delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary consent storage may cause the banner to ask for your choice again.

Other privacy signals

Magic Pixels does not sell personal information or use advertising tags. Browser-based opt-out signals therefore do not need to disable a sale or advertising activity on this site. We will review this if the site’s data practices change.

For more detail about personal-data processing and your rights, read the Privacy Policy.
