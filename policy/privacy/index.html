Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Magic Pixels is the controller for personal data processed through this website. This notice explains what happens when you visit magicpixels.shop.

What we process

Website delivery and security. Our hosting provider may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser, requested page and time of access to deliver and protect the site.

Consent choices. CookieYes stores the preferences you make in the cookie banner so the site can remember and respect them.

Optional analytics. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you explicitly accept Analytics cookies. If accepted, it may process page views, referring source, approximate location, browser and device information, and interactions such as clicks on affiliate product links. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or payment information to Analytics. Advertising storage, Google Signals and ad personalization remain disabled.

Affiliate links. When you follow a product link, the affiliate network and merchant receive the request and may process identifiers, device information and purchase attribution under their own privacy notices. Magic Pixels may receive a commission if a qualifying purchase is made. We do not receive your payment-card details from the merchant.

Why we process it

  • Site delivery, fraud prevention and security are necessary to operate the website and support our legitimate interests in keeping it available and secure.
  • Remembering your consent choice is necessary to respect that choice and meet our legal obligations.
  • Analytics is based on your consent. You can refuse it without losing access to the site and withdraw it at any time.
  • Affiliate attribution is initiated when you choose to follow a clearly disclosed affiliate link.

Providers and international transfers

We use GitHub Pages for hosting, CookieYes for consent management and, only after consent, Google Analytics for audience measurement. These providers may process data outside your country. Their contractual and legal safeguards apply to those transfers. Product merchants and affiliate networks act under their own privacy notices after you leave this site.

Retention

Technical logs, consent records and analytics data are retained according to the relevant provider settings and only for as long as needed for security, compliance, measurement and dispute handling. Aggregated reports that no longer identify an individual may be retained for longer. Withdrawing Analytics consent stops future Analytics collection from this site and removes accessible Analytics cookies from your browser.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of data, and withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. The site does not load advertising tags.

To exercise a privacy right or ask a privacy question, email Magic Pixels at magic.pixels.designs@gmail.com.

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children.
