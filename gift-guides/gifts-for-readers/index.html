Gifts for readers who already have plenty of books

The safest reader gift may not be a book. If you do not know what they own, what they have already read, or whether they prefer print, audio or digital, give something that supports the way they read.

Reading habitIdeaWhy it may fail
Borrows from the libraryRechargeable reading lightA clip can mark delicate covers; check its grip and charge method.
Carries a paperbackPadded book sleeveMeasure for their usual book size; large hardbacks need more room.
Owns and lends booksPersonal book embosserIt is less useful for someone who rarely keeps physical books.
Jots down favorite linesReading journalA highly structured journal may feel like homework.

Ask one quiet question if you can: “Do you mostly borrow, buy or listen?” That answer is more useful than asking for a favorite genre. For an audiobook listener, a sleeve or embosser has little value; a comfortable pair of headphones might help, but only if you know what device they use.

Avoid ordering a personalized stamp until you confirm the name and spelling. For lights, check battery life, brightness and whether the light works in their usual position. If you are still uncertain, pair a simple bookmark with a note about a book they once recommended to you.
