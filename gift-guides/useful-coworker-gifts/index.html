The Magic Pixels gift guide

Useful coworker gifts without the awkward joke

A coworker gift works best when it makes a workday a little easier without making assumptions about someone’s private life. Keep the price modest, follow any office exchange limit, and let a specific thank-you do the personal work.

IdeaWorks whenThink twice if
Pocket notebookThey like making quick listsTheir workflow is entirely digital.
Cable pouchThey commute with chargersYou do not know whether they carry tech.
Simple desk coasterDrinks sit at their deskThey work remotely or already have one.
Small plant-care accessoryYou have seen them tend office plantsThe workplace has no plants or storage space.

Choose something easy to carry home. Oversized mugs, scented products and inside jokes can be harder to use than they seem. Food can be welcome, but check allergies and dietary preferences first. A group gift is often better if the occasion is a major milestone and the team has an established approach.

For the note, name a real contribution: “Thank you for making the handoff so clear last month” means more than a generic compliment. If a gift feels too personal for your relationship, the note alone may be the better choice.
