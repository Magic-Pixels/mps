The Magic Pixels gift guide

Thoughtful gifts under a $25 planning budget

A small budget can still lead to a personal gift. Use what you know about one routine rather than trying to buy the biggest-looking object for the money. A note about why you chose it often does more work than elaborate packaging.

InterestSmall starting ideaCheck first
ReadingBookmark or reading notebookWhether they read physical books and use notes.
CookingIllustrated tea towelFabric and wash instructions.
PlantsReusable seed labelsMaterial and how to write on them.
DrawingPocket sketchbookPaper suitable for their preferred pens or paints.

The $25 band in the finder is a planning guide, not a current product price. Retailer results can cost more, especially after delivery, taxes or currency differences. Check the final total in the seller’s checkout and leave room in your budget for shipping. If the price only works with a large minimum order, it may not be the right gift.

When you cannot match an interest, make the gift an occasion: a favorite snack and an invitation to meet, a printed photo with a note, or a homemade recipe card. If you buy an object, choose one that is easy to use and easy to return if it misses the mark.
